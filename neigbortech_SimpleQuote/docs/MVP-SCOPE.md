# MVP scope and acceptance checks

The MVP is successful when a business owner can:

1. receive a lead from the public lead form;
2. accept a booking against real Google Calendar availability;
3. see both records in one dashboard;
4. create a pre-filled quote from either source;
5. edit line items and save the quote;
6. mark it accepted and convert it to an invoice;
7. record an amount paid and see the remaining balance;
8. print or save a branded PDF;
9. create a quote or invoice without a lead/booking;
10. customize business identity, colors, terms, numbering, tax, and payment instructions.

Out of scope for this milestone:

- centrally hosted multi-business SaaS;
- staff roles and permissions;
- payment processing;
- automated email/SMS delivery;
- accounting integrations;
- AI that writes records without confirmation.
